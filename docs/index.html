RESTIC · NAS · SYSTEMD

A second copy of your Restic repository.Automatically.

restic-nas-backup cleanly stops rest-server, mirrors the repository to a NAS share, then starts it again. Scheduling is configurable by day and time.

01

Protection against wrong destinations

The script verifies that /mnt/restic is a real CIFS mount matching the configured NAS source before writing anything.

02

Clean rest-server shutdown

The service stops during replication and restarts automatically, including when an error occurs.

03

Configurable scheduling

Choose the time and days directly with restic-nas schedule set DAYS TIME. No manual systemd editing is required.

ARCHITECTURE

Simple to understand. Simple to restore.

The NAS contains a usable copy of the repository. Once the share is mounted, Restic can read snapshots directly from that copy.
